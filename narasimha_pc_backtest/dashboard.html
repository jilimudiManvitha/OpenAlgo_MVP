Execution assumptions and interpretation

Both signal and forming entry candles must have no lower wick; an upper wick is optional. The completed signal high exceeds its upper band and VWAP. Only its immediate next minute can enter, when the market price exceeds the signal high, current upper band and current VWAP. Entry does not wait for candle close. The entry candle may develop a lower wick later; those trades are retained.

HA carries across observed sessions; Bollinger uses 20 HA closes and population standard deviation. VWAP resets daily and uses raw typical price × volume. Each minute follows the selected OHLC path with uniform modeled volume. Entry is sampled within each leg then refined; these are sensitivity scenarios, not exact ticks or guaranteed performance bounds. Stops and square-off use market prices with adverse slippage. Targets are resting limits. Fees are an illustrative flat 5 bps of each fill, not broker-specific historical taxes. Slippage is 5 bps on market fills plus tick rounding. Gross P&L already includes slippage; net also subtracts fees.

F&O status and ticks use the current local symbol master for the entire history. Historical membership/tick changes, adjustments, delisted companies, auctions, circuit limits and liquidity constraints are not reconstructed. This is the supplied present-day basket, with survivorship bias. Sessions missing any minute from 09:15 through square-off are excluded. Missing whole dates are only detectable against other source symbols, not an authoritative exchange calendar. No volume filter, leverage, compounding, trailing stop or indicator exit is added. Concurrent trades each receive up to ₹1 lakh; portfolio cash is not constrained. Daily-close drawdown omits intraday losses.
