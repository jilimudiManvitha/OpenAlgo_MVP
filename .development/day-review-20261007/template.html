Coverage & assumptions

Execution, costs and capital definitions
  • Uses the installed long and short strategy signal/fill/exit functions and the updated 12-variant options engine. All options entries require same-expiry, equal-quantity 200-point wings.
  • Equity uses one-minute candles with eight interpolated samples per path segment, proportional volume, 5 basis points adverse entry/stop/market-exit slippage and tick rounding. Fixed targets use the installed target fill model. Five-minute candles form causally from minute observations. No order latency, liquidity queue, partial fill or market-impact simulation.
  • Options choose strikes using the previous completed minute and fill at the next open with ₹0.05 adverse slippage and tick rounding. Greeks are estimated Black-76 values; historical broker Greeks are unavailable. Capital stops include the same FYERS fee estimates used by this report.
  • Each options strategy has ₹20 lakh allocated. Sizing reserves (200 points + 3% spot + long premiums) × quantity, divided by 0.90 for the cash reserve. The strategy detail shows the largest modeled basket reserve. This is not historical SPAN. Equity allocates up to ₹10,000 entry value per trade without a shared portfolio-wide cap.
  • Realized net = closed gross P&L − closed round-trip charges. Total marked net = realized net + gross open P&L − open entry charges. No hypothetical future exit fee is charged to an open position. Charge components cover brokerage, STT, exchange, SEBI, stamp, IPFT, clearing and GST; displayed paise rounding can differ from contract notes.
  • FYERS standard brokerage: intraday lower of 0.03% or ₹20 per executed order; options ₹20 per executed order. Rates were checked October 7. Excludes account, financing, call-and-trade, broker auto-square-off and exercise/assignment charges. No production funds are debited by this estimate. FYERS official charges · NSE STT schedule.
  • End-of-day scanner membership and the saved Wednesday list are applied retrospectively. This introduces selection bias. The one-day positional test starts with no previous holdings, so it does not reconstruct actual carried-account P&L. No results are written into application Reports.
  • Peak capital aggregates exposures at their timestamps; it is not the sum of each strategy's separate peak. Total entry value instead adds every entry, including reused capital. A one-day return cannot establish sustainable profitability or a longer calendar streak.
