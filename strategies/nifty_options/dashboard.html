How to read the report

Trade count: one completed cycle from initial entry until all legs exit, including rebalances and condor re-entries in that cycle. Individual CE, PE and hedge exits are separate leg records. Open cycles are excluded from trade win statistics.

Win rate: profitable completed cycles ÷ all completed cycles. Profit factor: total winning-cycle net P&L ÷ absolute losing-cycle net P&L. Win/loss payoff: average winning cycle ÷ average losing cycle. Expectancy: mean net P&L of completed cycles. Fees are already included. Zero-P&L cycles count as breakeven.

Net P&L: includes marked open positions and their entry costs. Completed-cycle P&L can therefore differ. Sharpe and Sortino use daily returns, 252 sessions/year and zero risk-free rate. Infinity means profitable cycles with no losing cycles; N/A means no defined ratio.

Assumptions, coverage and risk limits

OLHC and OHLC assume different orderings of each one-minute candle's prices. They are separate simulations, not observed ticks or guaranteed bounds. Signals use prior-minute observations and fills use subsequent opens or modeled stop crossings. Historical deltas, margins and fees are estimates. Costs: ₹20 plus 0.1% turnover per fill and ₹0.05 adverse slippage. Missing held bars delay monitoring; unobserved prices are not filled. The strategy detail shows each affected minute count.

The ₹20,000 stop is a cycle-loss trigger, not a guaranteed maximum loss or a trailing equity stop. Gaps and costs may exceed it; drawdowns can span several cycles. Open positions at October 1 are marked, not force-closed. Aggregated P&L is an arithmetic sum of separate strategy simulations, not a shared-account execution or margin test. Three months is a short historical sample.
