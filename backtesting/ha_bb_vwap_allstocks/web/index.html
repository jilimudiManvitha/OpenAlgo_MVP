Metric definitions and assumptions
Capital used is remaining gross entry notional across open positions, including shorts. It is not broker margin. Cumulative entry notional and turnover reuse capital. Average capital and open trades are time-weighted over the recorded regular sessions.
Max win/loss means the largest completed trade after modeled fees and slippage. Period drawdown follows the portfolio across days and includes within-day bar-close drawdown; it is not the largest daily drawdown alone. Intrabar drawdowns can be larger. Each strategy/path is independent.
Calendar quarter: Jan–Mar, Apr–Jun, Jul–Sep, Oct–Dec. Six months: Jan–Jun or Jul–Dec. Five-year groups are anchored to the configured starting year. Periods use only committed sessions and disclose missing coverage.
