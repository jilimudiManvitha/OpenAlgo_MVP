* **Execution:**
* If the market breaks out in one direction (e.g., breaks the day's high), sell only the **Put option** first[17][18].
* If the breakout fails or the market starts reversing, write a matching **Call option** to lock in a delta-neutral, premium-matched strangle[18].
* **Deep OTM Strikes:** Sell extremely safe, far Out-of-the-Money (OTM) options with a tiny premium of around **₹10** (which typically have a very low delta of \~0.06)[19][20].
* **Advanced Stop Loss:** Set a wide stop loss of **300% (4 times the initial premium)**[20]. A ₹10 premium must touch **₹40** before you exit
To protect against catastrophic overnight gap risk (like a sudden 10% market movement) and optimize capital, the speaker converts their short strangle into an **Iron Condor**[2].

* **The Setup:** Sell your ₹10 OTM Call and Put, then buy cheaper, protective OTM options **150 to 200 points further out** on both sides[3][24].
* **Key Benefits:**
  1. **Capped Risk:** Converts unlimited potential loss into a strictly capped maximum loss (e.g., ₹12,300 per lot)[2][24].
  2. **Margin Reduction:** Slashes the capital requirement per lot from roughly **₹2 Lakhs down to ₹75,000**

### **2\. Setup &amp; Strike Selection (4-Leg Breakdown)**

Under normal market conditions, the strategy involves selling Out-of-the-Money (OTM) options and buying further OTM options as protection[3][6]:

1. **Sell OTM Call Option**: Select a Call strike roughly **500 points above spot** (trading around a **₹10 premium**)[5][7].
2. **Sell OTM Put Option**: Select a Put strike roughly **500 points below spot** (trading around a **₹10–₹12 premium**)[5][7].
3. **Buy Call Protection (Hedge)**: Buy a Call strike **200 points further OTM** from your sold Call (around a **₹4 premium**)[6].
4. **Buy Put Protection (Hedge)**: Buy a Put strike **200 points further OTM** from your sold Put (around a **₹4–₹6 premium**)[6].
5. **Net Credit**: Collecting \~₹6 net credit on the Call side and \~₹6 net credit on the Put side yields a **total net credit of \~₹12**, targeting roughly **1% profit per week** on deployed capital[5].

---

### **3\. Capital &amp; Margin Benefits**

* **Margin Requirement**: Selling naked strangle legs requires \~₹2,00,000 in margin[4][6]. Adding 200-point protection hedges cuts the margin requirement by more than half to approximately **₹88,000–₹1,00,000**[6].
* **Risk Capping**: Buying protection hedges defines and caps your maximum potential loss if a sudden gap-up or gap-down occurs[8].

---

### **4\. Impact of India VIX**

* **Low VIX (\~11)**: Option premiums are lower, so sold strikes are typically selected around **500 points away** from spot[12][13].
* **High VIX (15–16)**: Option premiums expand due to higher volatility, allowing you to select strikes further out (**up to 1,000 points away**) while collecting similar premiums[12][13].

---

### **5\. Risk Management &amp; Stop Loss Rules**

* **Golden Rule (Portfolio Stop Loss)**: If your total P&amp;L shows a **\-1% loss** on deployed capital (e.g., -₹1,000 loss on ₹1,00,000 capital), **close all 4 legs immediately** and exit for the week[11][14].
* **Individual Leg Threshold**: If a sold option premium (sold at \~₹10) doubles or triples to **₹20–₹30**, it signals that the position needs an adjustment or exit[14].

---

### **6\. Adjustment Techniques**

If Nifty trends towards one side and pushes a sold option premium to ₹20–₹30, two primary adjustment methods can be applied:

* **Method 1 (Beginner/Simple Adjustment)**: Square off the troubled leg. Sell a new OTM option further away whose premium matches the remaining safe leg's current premium (\~₹5–₹6)[15][16].
* **Method 2 (Advanced Re-balancing)**: Square off the initial legs and re-open a fresh Iron Condor by selling new \~₹10 Call and Put options with 200-point hedges[17]. This shifts the range with market movement to keep positions delta-neutral[21][22].
